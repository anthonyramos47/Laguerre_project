Registered names (vaults, shells, parametric surfaces) are fitted with the values in the table.
      Change any of them in the notebook with params.update(...).

    
Random families shell_<seed>, roof_<seed> and loft_<seed> are rebuilt from their seed,
      so any seed works, not only the ones shown here. Roofs and lofts are K > 0 for every seed; a random shell is cut to its K > 0 band.

    
u, v ranges are in the function's own parameters and are mapped to the B-spline's [0, 1]².
      Tubes and shells are K > 0 only on their outer band.
